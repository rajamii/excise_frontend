The aforesaid license has been granted as per the following terms and conditions:

I. The licensee shall purchase foreign liquor (excluding draught beer and liquor imported from outside India) only from such licensees of the district as may be specified by the Collector.  

II. The license permits sale of loose foreign liquor for consumption on the premises.  

III. The licensee shall not sell and stock Foreign Liquor which is unfit for human consumption.  

IV. The maximum quantity that may be possessed at a time under the license shall be as fixed by the District Excise Officer.  

V. The licensee shall stock all foreign liquor other than malt liquor (beer) and wine, in sealed bottles, each containing at least 700 milliliter, except that ones that have been open for sale. However, except draught beer, malt liquor (beer) and wine can/shall be stocked in cans or glass bottles of the capacity of 325 milliliter or more.  

VI. The licensee shall preserve all the permits and invoices of stock received in proper order and produce them for inspection.  

VII. The licensee shall maintain day‑to‑day true account of all foreign liquor received, stocked and sold.  

VIII. The licensee shall be personally responsible for the due observance of the license conditions.  

IX. The licensee shall submit a return incorporating all the relevant details about purchase, sales, balance etc. within 3 days of the expiry of the license.  

X. On breach of any condition of the license or provisions of the Sikkim Excise Act, 1992 or rules made thereunder or orders issued by the Excise Commissioner, this license shall be suspended or cancelled by the Licensing Authority.
